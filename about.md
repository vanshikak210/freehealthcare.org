Too many people already qualify for coverage they never receive — not because they were turned away, but because no one ever explained it to them in language they could use. We exist to close that gap.

## How we work

- **We translate.** Guides and workshops that explain coverage without jargon.
- **We connect.** Pointing people toward the free and low-cost care they're entitled to.
- **We advocate.** Campaigning for billing rules patients can actually understand.

## Our status

Free Healthcare Initiative of California is a chapter of Free Healthcare Initiative, which holds federal 501(c)(3) nonprofit status. Donations made through us support that organization's mission and are tax-deductible under its status.

We serve families across all 58 California counties.
