### Need help right now?
- **Medical emergency:** call [911](tel:911).
- **Mental health crisis:** call or text [988](tel:988) — free, 24 hours a day.
- **Free Medicare counseling in California:** HICAP, [1-800-434-0222](tel:18004340222).
